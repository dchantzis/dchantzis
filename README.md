## Engineering Philosophy

I believe good engineering starts with understanding the problem, the people affected by it and the operational environment in which a system needs to work.

I favour pragmatic solutions delivered in small, secure and testable increments. Breaking larger initiatives into manageable phases allows users to benefit earlier, reduces operational overhead sooner and creates opportunities for feedback to shape subsequent improvements.

I continue learning from live operations, production behaviour and how people actually use software. Understanding real workflows, exceptions and manual workarounds helps me identify problems that aren't always apparent during initial requirements gathering or system design.

I balance delivery speed with reliability, maintainability and long-term architectural considerations. My focus is on building dependable systems that simplify operations, reduce unnecessary manual work and deliver measurable business value.

I value engineering practices that support these goals, rather than applying patterns, frameworks or architectural complexity for their own sake.
